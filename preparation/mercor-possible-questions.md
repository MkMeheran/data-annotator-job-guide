# Mercor: Questions That Could Be Asked

This is a practice list. It is not an official list from Mercor, and I do not claim that these exact questions were asked. I made it from the type of work in the assessment and from what annotation jobs usually need. Use it to practice speaking and writing your answers.

## About the job and you

- What does a data annotator do, in your own words?
  Hint: label or check data so AI models learn correctly.
- Why do you want this role?
  Hint: connect it to your skills, like attention to detail and written English.
- Tell me about a time you found your own mistake.
  Hint: say what happened, how you found it, and what you changed.

## Accuracy and guidelines

- What do you do if the guideline does not cover a case?
  Hint: flag it as low-confidence or ask, do not guess.
- What do you do when you disagree with the guideline?
  Hint: follow it, and report the problem through the right channel.
- Speed or accuracy, which is more important?
  Hint: accuracy first, then speed with a good method.
- How do you stay consistent across many similar tasks?
  Hint: use a checklist, take short breaks, and review your own work.

## Scenario questions

- You find that a batch you finished earlier has an error. What do you do?
  Hint: report it quickly, fix what you can, and say how you will avoid it.
- Two annotators label the same item in different ways. What should happen?
  Hint: go back to the guideline, and ask a reviewer if it is still unclear.
- You are not sure about a label and the deadline is near. What do you do?
  Hint: use the low-confidence flag, and do not invent an answer.

## Test-related questions

- How did you check your work in the assessment?
  Hint: be honest. Say what you did and what you would improve.
- How would you count words with a rule, without making mistakes?
  Hint: write the rule down, one pass per rule, count twice.
- How would you summarize a long text in a small word limit?
  Hint: find key facts, keep numbers exact, add nothing, check the word count.

## Basic knowledge

- Why does bad labeled data hurt an AI model?
  Hint: the model learns the wrong patterns and gives wrong results.
- What is the difference between classification and bounding boxes?
  Hint: classification gives a label to the whole item, boxes mark where an object is.
- What does a reviewer do?
  Hint: checks annotators' work and catches errors and inconsistency.

## Practical questions

- How many hours can you work, and in which time zone?
- Do you have stable internet and a quiet place?
- Can you follow a daily or hourly quality check?
