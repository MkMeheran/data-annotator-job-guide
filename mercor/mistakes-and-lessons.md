# Mercor Test: My Mistakes and What I Should Have Done

I wrote this after I submitted the test and checked my own work. I do not give the real answers. I only show where the thinking went wrong.

## Section 1: counting

What went wrong:
- I counted by eye and typed the numbers fast.
- I did not write the rules down before counting.
- None of my five counts matched when I checked later.
- Some questions have two conditions at once, for example "starts with a vowel" and "longer than 5 letters". It is hard to hold both in your head.

What I should have done:
- Paste the text into a plain editor and write each rule in my own words.
- Make one pass through the text for each question.
- Count twice, and a third time if the two numbers are different.
- Read the final number against the question before moving on. The passage is full of numbers, so it is easy to type one of them by mistake.

## Section 2: strings

- I marked one pair as SAME when it was DIFFERENT. One letter in the middle was changed.
- Next time I will split both strings into chunks of 4 or 5 letters and compare chunk by chunk, from the left and again from the right.
- I will be more careful, not less, with a pair that looks the same.

## The summary

I checked it with four IELTS writing criteria.

**Task response**
- It was a little over the 150-word limit.
- Two sentences changed the meaning of the passage.
- I added ideas that the passage never said.
- I left out important points and exact details.

**Coherence and cohesion**
- No opening sentence to tell the reader the topic.
- The ideas jumped around and did not follow the passage.
- Linking words like "as a result" were used where cause and result were not clear.

**Lexical resource**
- I repeated the same words many times.
- Some word choices were wrong or unnatural, such as "unitedly" and "work sample count".

**Grammar**
- Subject-verb agreement mistakes, missing prepositions, and sentences that started with a small letter.

Examples from my own writing:

| --- | --- |

| annotators have to work in 1.18 pm with the central server | Annotators must connect to the central server at 01:18 PM. |
| Excessive temperatures leads image rendering exactly for 28 seconds | Rendering can lag when the server room becomes too hot. |

## Next time

1. Write the rules down before counting.
2. Count twice.
3. Compare strings in chunks.
4. For a summary: mark key facts, write in the order of the passage, keep the numbers exact, add nothing, check the word count, then read once for meaning and once for grammar.

Most of my mistakes did not come from a lack of knowledge. They came from not checking.
